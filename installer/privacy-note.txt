OpenDM: what this program does on your computer and online

No telemetry, no ads, no account. OpenDM sends nothing to its developers.

Online, OpenDM connects only to:
- the websites of the downloads you start;
- github.com and gyan.dev, to download yt-dlp and FFmpeg for video downloads, and only
  after you agree (you see the sizes and the folder first);
- github.com for the update check, only if you turn it on (it is off until you choose it in
  the first-run wizard or Settings -> Updates).

On your computer, setup:
- installs the programs into the folder you choose, for your Windows account only;
- registers the browser connection for Chrome and Edge (two registry keys under your
  account; Settings -> Browser can remove them);
- keeps settings, history and logs in %LOCALAPPDATA%\OpenDM. Cookies from your browser are
  stored encrypted and sent only to the site they belong to. Logs never contain link
  tokens, cookies or passwords.

Uninstalling removes the programs, the video tools and the browser connection, and asks
whether to delete your settings and history. Your downloaded files are never deleted.

Full privacy policy: https://github.com/Indrajit147/OpenDM/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md
